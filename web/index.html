Dataset Integrity & Clinical Schema

Target Formulation & Class Proportions

The canonical UCI Cleveland diagnosis variable (num) contains integers 0 through 4. Value 0 denotes absence of significant coronary narrowing (<50% diameter), whereas values 1–4 denote presence of angiographic disease. Target leakage is prevented by dropping num entirely and fitting all transformers within cross-validation folds.

Absence (Class 0): 164 (54.1%) Presence (Class 1): 139 (45.9%)
Missing Values Resolved: Identified and converted ? placeholders in ca (4 missing) and thal (2 missing). Median & mode imputation applied exclusively within training splits.

Phase 6: Clinical Error Diagnostics

Test Set Error Analysis (7 Misclassified Cases)

Examining the 7 patients out of 61 test cases that were incorrectly classified by the champion model.

False Negatives (2 Patients) - Critical Risk

Test Indices #165 & #279: Patients who possessed true angiographic heart disease (>50% vessel narrowing) but were predicted as Absence (0).

Diagnostic Investigation: Both patients presented with atypical non-anginal symptoms, low ST depression (< 1.0 mm), and normal fluoroscopy vessels (ca=0.0). Their non-invasive test profiles mimic healthy patients, illustrating why non-invasive ML cannot replace definitive angiography.

False Positives (5 Patients) - False Alarms

Test Indices #137, #139, #163, #190, #252: Patients with absence of significant narrowing (<50%) who were flagged as high risk (Presence).

Diagnostic Investigation: These patients exhibited severe isolated risk factors (e.g. resting blood pressure > 160 mm Hg, elderly age > 65, or abnormal ECG ST-T waves). In clinical practice, false alarms lead to confirmatory secondary stress testing rather than missed intervention.
